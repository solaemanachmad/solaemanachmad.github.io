Publications

2025

A Systematic Literature Review of Transfer Learning for Eye Tracking

Achmad Solaeman, Syukron Abu Ishaq Alfarozi, Sunu Wibirama

ICADEIS 2025 · Conference paper · IEEE · pp. 1-7 · doi:10.1109/ICADEIS65852.2025.10933367

A review of 29 studies (2018–2024) showing how transfer learning addresses data scarcity and individual variability in gaze classification and estimation, with best practices for preprocessing and fine-tuning.

2024

Robust Fetal Head Circumference Calculation on Ultrasound Images Using Just-in-Time Compiled Ellipse Fitting

Pandega Abyan Zumarsyah, Achmad Solaeman, Fhareza Alvindo, Hanung Adi Nugroho

TENCON 2024 · Conference paper · IEEE · pp. 161-164 · doi:10.1109/TENCON61640.2024.10903012

A fast, memory-efficient method based on JIT-compiled Randomized Hough Transform ellipse fitting, reaching Dice 0.93 and Hausdorff 3.88 mm in 0.41 s per image with under 800 kB of memory.
